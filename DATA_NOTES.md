# Synthetic Data Notes

## Provenance

Every learner-facing organisation, headline, and record in Signal & Story is fictional. The datasets were authored specifically for Taskmarket TSK-5DV55M47. They are not measurements of real people, products, schools, communities, or scientific effects.

The runtime source of learner-facing metadata is `data/datasets.json`. Deterministic constructed records for sampling and correlation are generated by pure functions in `src/model.js`, so tests and repeated fixed-seed cases are reproducible.

## Dataset 1 — Nova Arcade queue board

**Purpose:** demonstrate that changing a chart's vertical-axis range changes rendering/emphasis without changing the data or computed statistics.

**Unit:** seconds.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `night` | Fictional event-night sequence label. |
| `waitSeconds` | Synthetic average match wait for that event night, in seconds. |

Records: 82, 80, 79, 77, 75, 74 seconds across Nights 1–6. The app always computes the same mean (77.8 s), range (74–82 s), and first-to-last change (-8 s). The axis slider only changes SVG mapping.

## Dataset 2 — Orbit Puzzle Room exit ratings

**Purpose:** contrast a plausibly biased sampling frame with a less-biased frame and show why a larger biased sample need not fix coverage bias.

**Population:** 240 synthetic visit records; exactly 60 in each of four time blocks.

**Unit:** rating on a 1–5 scale.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | Synthetic visit identifier, not a person identifier. |
| `timeBlock` | Opening hour, Mid-session, Late session, or Closing hour. |
| `rating` | Synthetic exit rating on a 1–5 scale. |

Construction intentionally gives later blocks higher average ratings. The full population mean is 3.45. The **closing-hour intercept** samples randomly only from Closing hour, so the other blocks have zero selection probability. The **all-window stratified** method draws randomly within every time block; because population strata are equal-sized, it draws equal counts from each.

Sampling uses the documented Mulberry32 seeded pseudorandom generator. Seed 2026 at n=24 gives a closing-only mean of 4.23 and an all-window mean of 3.47; the exact selected IDs repeat for the same seed.

## Dataset 3 — Skyline Puzzle League sessions

**Purpose:** demonstrate association, stratification, and a plausible third-variable explanation without claiming causality.

**Population:** 45 synthetic sessions; 15 each for Easy, Medium, and Hard puzzle difficulty.

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `session` | Synthetic session identifier. |
| `difficulty` | Invented puzzle difficulty group. |
| `hints` | Number of hints used in the synthetic session. |
| `seconds` | Synthetic completion time in seconds. |

Harder puzzle groups are constructed with both more hints and higher base completion times. Within each difficulty band, more hints are constructed to be associated with somewhat shorter times. Result: overall Pearson r = 0.826, while each difficulty stratum has r = -0.881. This is an instructional construction showing why an overall association can change after conditioning on a third variable. It does not demonstrate a real causal mechanism.

## Fresh transfer case — BrightByte Games

The fictional headline and three facts are text-only synthetic prompts. They combine: a narrow 50–56 axis for a 51→55 score change; a tournament-final-only sampling frame used to discuss all players; and session count as a plausible third variable connecting scoreboard use and replay interest.

No value in the transfer case is presented as a real finding.
